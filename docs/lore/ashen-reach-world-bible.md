## Originality Covenant

Ashen Reach is an all-original LitD world. It uses generic fantasy and game
structure where those ideas are common to the genre: six attributes, familiar
class archetypes, generic ancestry categories, alignment and ethos pressure,
full-loot conflict, faction warfare, scarcity, aging, and permanent death.

All protectable creative expression is original here: world names, place names,
god names, faction identities, titles, relic names, written lore, rituals,
history, and prose. Do not copy protected text, named lore, maps, characters, or
distinct expressive presentation from another game or setting. Mechanics may
echo classic permadeath PvP-roleplay depth; the world and expression remain
ours.

## Setting

The realm is Vhael, a war-scarred continent where death is permanent and a
person's name is the only thing that can outlive the body. The free city of
Galdmere is the crossroads capital, neutral ground, and home of the public
Mourning Wall where names of the remembered are carved.

The Ashen Reach is the grey wound left by the Sundering, a half-real frontier
where the Weave is thin and the Hollow leaks through. Dangerous magic is easier
there, relics surface there, and reputations are made or erased there.

## Metaphysics

Vhael hangs between the Weave and the Hollow. The Weave is living order: breath,
stone, memory, law, and oath. The Hollow is the Outer Dark: a starless outside
where nothing holds its shape and bargains always cost more than they promise.

Power has three sources:

| Source | Meaning | World Use |
|---|---|---|
| Invocation | Mortal force drawn through the Weave. | Mage-class spellcraft and elemental practice. |
| Supplication | Power lent by a god to a proven servant. | Priest empowerment and divine favors. |
| Pact | Forbidden bargains with Hollow denizens. | Hollow Pact magic and corruption systems. |

Death is final because the Weave remembers a life instead of re-knitting it. The
dead return only as memory, monument, story, consequence, or Hollow-tainted
wrongness. The central question of the world is: by what name will you be
mourned?

Each faction is alignment or ethos gated, AI-led through the agent systems,
ranked through measured roleplay quality, and built for item-of-power warfare:
steal a rival relic to its shrine to strip powers; defend your own; forge
temporary alliances when politics demands it.

one god through sustained roleplay, service, sacrifice, and witnessed conduct.
The gods may be embodied by AI, by human Immortals, or by co-embodiment where a
human admin can take over without breaking continuity.

## Peoples

Common fantasy ancestries may exist as generic game categories. Their local
cultures are original to Vhael: hill-dwarves under Karthspire, wood-elves of
the Thornwilds, orc warbands near the Reach, city-born humans of Galdmere, and
wandering mixed-ancestry houses moving between factions.

Original kindreds:

| Kindred | Identity |
|---|---|
| Sylphkin | Wind-born, feathered folk of the high crags. |
| Felisar | Lithe cat-kin of the southern wastes. |
| Ashkin | Hollow-touched grey elves marked by the Sundering and gifted in thin-Weave places. |
| Saur | Scale-folk of the drowned coasts and brackish ruins. |

The broader NPC population should feel varied and human: brave, craven, capable,
weak, wise, foolish, generous, petty, beautiful, scarred, and ordinary. Champions
and leaders carry the heroic visual identity; everyday citizens should still
feel socially and psychologically real.

## Mourning and Legacy

Death is permanent, aging matters, and hoarded power decays into risk. The
Mourning Wall is not a scoreboard; it is the social soul of Vhael. The living
decide who is remembered through songs, eulogies, grudges, official histories,
and faction propaganda. A forgotten death is the true failure.

Gameplay systems tied to this root include constitution, age, permanent death,
corpse looting, relic decay, memorialization, eulogies from other players, and
the Hall of the Remembered.

## Legends, Relics, and Monsters

Ashen Dragons are the eldest monsters of Drakemarch: grey-scaled hoarders of
Sundering relics. To slay one should mark a character for an age.

Relics of the Sundering are rare, Hollow-touched artifacts with one or two
instances in existence. Root examples: Mourncrown, Hollowed Blade, and Lantern
That Does Not Gutter. They should make their bearers powerful and therefore
hunted.

The Hollow's Children are wrong shapes that slip through thin places:
Veilwalkers, Greylings, and worse things near the Reach.

The First Mourned are legendary figures whose names still anchor bright scars in
the Weave. Their tombs, gifts, and unfinished oaths form dungeon and quest roots.

## Visual Identity Notes

Heroic male champions should read as masculine, powerful, and physically
commanding in roles of strength, command, and combat. Women should be presented
with feminine visual identity. The overall world uses traditional visual roles
for high-status archetypal art direction while preserving a broad, realistic NPC
population in personality and competence.
