# Ashen Reach Peoples, Mourning, And Legends

Canonical detail for issue #1059 under the #1055 world bible, #1056
cosmology, #1057 ages/gazetteer, and #1058 pantheon/faction canon. This
document defines Vhael's peoples, death and legacy customs, the Ashen Dragons,
Sundering relics, Hollow-spawned monsters, and legendary heroes whose names
still shape play.

## Originality Boundary

This is all-original LitD setting expression. The idea of fantasy ancestries,
permanent death, graves, memorials, dragons, rare artifacts, and dungeon heroes
is generic genre and game structure. The protected expression here is ours:
Vhael, Sylphkin, Felisar, Ashkin, Saur, the Mourning Wall, the First Mourned,
Ashen Dragons, Hollow's Children, relic names, customs, rites, and written
prose.

Do not copy protected race lore, dragon lore, tomb lore, artifact prose,
funerary customs, named factions, maps, characters, or quest text from another
setting. Outside material may only inform generic design pressure before new
Vhael-specific expression is written.

## Peoples Rule

Kindred choice is identity, social pressure, and play style. A kindred should
suggest stat tendencies, resistances, innate gifts, cultural hooks, faction
friction, and visual direction without becoming a stereotype or a lore excuse
to flatten individual characters.

Generic fantasy ancestries may exist as common game categories. Their Ashen
Reach cultures, names, conflicts, rites, and places are original to Vhael.
Unique kindreds use original Vhael names in player-facing canon.

## Character Creation Hooks

Character creation should expose kindreds as playable lives, not encyclopedia
entries. A useful kindred entry names:

| Hook | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Stat Shape | Strength, agility, endurance, perception, will, or social tendency. |
| Resistance | A concrete resilience or vulnerability tied to body, culture, or cosmology. |
| Innate Gift | A small always-readable power or authored advantage. |
| Social Pressure | Who mistrusts, courts, hunts, or honors the kindred and why. |
| Visual Cue | A silhouette, material, posture, or iconography note grounded in README art. |

No kindred should silently bypass alignment, ethos, faction, priest, or Hollow
Pact rules. Exceptions must be authored, visible, and verified.

## The Mourning Rule

Death is permanent. The Weave remembers a life instead of restoring it. The
living decide whether that memory becomes public honor, whispered scandal,
legal precedent, faction propaganda, or nothing at all.

The question "by what name will you be mourned?" is the center of Vhael. A
character is not measured by survival alone. A coward who hoards power and dies
unwitnessed may vanish. A reckless defender who dies buying time for strangers
may become a song, a wall mark, a banner, or a quest.

## Mourning Practices

| Practice | Meaning | System Hook |
|---|---|---|
| Mourning Wall | Public stone in Galdmere where remembered names are carved. | Memorial UI, eulogy records, public reputation. |
| Grave Rows | Local cemeteries and faction graveyards for bodies that can be recovered. | Corpse recovery, grave markers, faction rites. |
| Last Fire | Funeral torch rite where witnesses speak what the dead spent life on. | Player eulogies, NPC speeches, final conduct summary. |
| Vesper Ledger | Vesper Court record that can preserve, edit, or politically weaponize a name. | Lore surfaces, rumors, codex unlocks, reputation disputes. |
| Forgotten Ash | Unmourned deaths whose names thin until Hollow things can misuse them. | Hollow encounter hooks, lost-name quests, warning lore. |

Mourning is not a scoreboard. It is social reality. Who speaks, who lies, who
stays silent, and who pays for stone all matter.

## Permanent Death And Legacy

Aging, constitution loss, permanent death, corpse looting, memorialization, and
the seven-day decay of hoarded relic power all serve one thesis: power must be
used, names must be earned, and no inventory outlives meaning.

Corpse looting should be harsh but legible. Grave and memorial systems should
let the living interpret a death without undoing it. Relic decay should make
hoarded legendary power dangerous to keep, politically useful to reveal, and
tragic to lose.

Normal resurrection is forbidden drift. The dead return only as memory,
monument, story, consequence, or Hollow-tainted wrongness.

## Legends Rule

Legends are playable anchors. Every legend should provide at least one dungeon,
relic, faction claim, monster threat, memorial custom, or AI co-authoring seed.
A legend that cannot change play is decorative and should be tightened until it
does.

Treasure routes, tomb directions, and dragon lairs must respect #944. A fresh
RPG character gets no free player map, minimap, or omniscient world-map screen.
Directions come from diegetic map items, cartographers, rumors, signs,
landmarks, journals, or learned knowledge.

## Ashen Dragons

Ashen Dragons are the eldest known monsters of Drakemarch. Their scales are
grey with Reach-ash, their eyes burn like banked coals under storm cloud, and
their hoards are layered with Sundering relics, stolen names, broken banners,
and trophies from dead ages.

They are not ordinary beasts with large health bars. Each Ashen Dragon should
have:

| Requirement | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Hoard Thesis | What kind of relic, name, or history the dragon covets. |
| Lair Pressure | Weather, terrain, minions, memory hazard, or Hollow scar. |
| Social Consequence | Which faction wants it dead, bargained with, or awakened. |
| Mourning Outcome | What happens to the killer's name if the hunt is witnessed. |

To slay one should mark a character for an age. To die to one should still be
rememberable if the death had witness, courage, or consequence.

## Relics Of The Sundering

Sundering relics are rare, Hollow-touched artifacts with one or two instances
in existence. They grant real power and create real danger.

| Relic | Identity | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Mourncrown | A broken gold crown that makes witnesses remember the bearer. | Rivals, tyrants, and Hollow things can find the name more easily. |
| Hollowed Blade | A dark sword that cuts through armor and weak names alike. | Each oath-broken kill thins the wielder's own remembered name. |
| Lantern That Does Not Gutter | A violet-lit lantern that burns in rain, tombs, and Hollow wind. | It reveals wrongness, but wrongness can also see it. |
| Bell Of Last Return | A handbell that calls the last honest words spoken near a corpse. | It cannot restore the dead and may expose a truth no faction wanted public. |

Several relics in one life should make a character powerful enough to matter
and hunted enough to suffer. Relics must be persisted as specific world facts,
not generic loot tiers wearing famous names.

## Hollow's Children

Hollow's Children are wrong shapes that seep through thin Weave. They are not a
single species. They are ways the outside learns to imitate hunger, grief, and
memory.

| Horror | Sign | Play Pressure |
|---|---|---|
| Veilwalkers | Tall quiet silhouettes moving behind mist or funeral cloth. | Stalk mourners, cross thin boundaries, punish careless graves. |
| Greylings | Small ash-skinned scavengers with too many remembered voices. | Pack threats, relic thieves, lost-name lures. |
| Nameleeches | Pale crawling things that feed on epitaphs and legal records. | Attack memorial systems, archives, and forgotten graves. |
| Lantern Wights | Corpses animated by false light around a stolen last wish. | Punish fake resurrection, expose Hollow-tainted wrongness. |

Hollow horrors should feel like pressure on meaning. They do not merely kill;
they misname, erase, mimic, and bargain.

## The First Mourned

The First Mourned are legendary heroes of past ages whose names still anchor
bright scars in the Weave. Their tombs are dungeons, their unfinished promises
are quests, and their gifts are tests of legacy.

| Name | Memory | Play Hook |
|---|---|---|
| Ser Caldrin Vale | First knight whose funeral oath held a city gate through one winter night. | Tomb defense dungeon, shield relic, Dawnward and Iron Verdict dispute. |
| Maerith Of The Seven Bells | Vesper singer who named the dead during the first Hollow storm. | Bell archive, eulogy mechanics, Vael Mourn memory quest. |
| Brannoc Rootsworn | Dwarf oath-smith who sealed a Karthspire under-road with his own name. | Forge vault, anti-Hollow ward, Ironbound craft hook. |
| Ysra Stormfeather | Sylphkin scout who carried warning over Drakemarch ash and never landed. | High-crag trial, wind rite, Ashen Dragon route rumor. |

The First Mourned are not invulnerable mascots. Their memories can be disputed,
forged, weaponized, or recovered by living players.

## Forbidden Drift

Do not make kindreds into copied race lore. Do not use stale unique-kindred
names as player-facing Ashen Reach canon. Do not make permanent death cosmetic.
Do not add normal resurrection. Do not make the Mourning Wall a leaderboard. Do
not turn Ashen Dragons into generic bosses. Do not make Sundering relics common
loot. Do not make Hollow horrors ordinary undead. Do not give fresh RPG
characters a free map, minimap, or omniscient world-map screen.
