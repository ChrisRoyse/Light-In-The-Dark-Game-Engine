# Ashen Reach Pantheon And Factions

Canonical detail for issue #1058 under the #1055 world bible, #1056 cosmology,
and #1057 ages/gazetteer canon. This document defines the seven gods, priest
empowerment rules, seven factions, faction gates, items of power, leader titles,
AI/human embodiment boundaries, and item-of-power warfare.

## Originality Boundary

This is all-original LitD setting expression. The idea of gods with domains,
factions with alignment gates, holy orders, courts, empires, wildfolk, and
forbidden cults is generic genre or game structure. The protected expression
here is ours: the names, titles, doctrines, relics, rites, faction identities,
leader titles, and written prose of Vhael.

Do not copy protected pantheons, order names, faction iconography, relic names,
quest text, prayers, hierarchy titles, or political histories from another
setting. Outside references may only inform generic design pressure before new
Vhael-specific expression is written.

## Pantheon Rule

The gods are active sources of pressure, not lore cards. Each god must be able
to favor, test, withhold, or forsake a mortal in ways that are visible to play.
Supplication is borrowed power from a divine will; it cannot be stolen, bought
from a trainer, or faked by ordinary Invocation.

Gods may be embodied by AI, by human Immortals, or by co-embodiment where a
human admin takes over without breaking continuity. The source of truth is the
god's doctrine and observed conduct, not the operator behind the voice.

## God Details

Korthac honors strength that survives witness. He does not bless slaughter that
cannot be named, and he scorns triumph hidden behind lies. His priests are rare
on quiet roads but common where a battle will decide the memory of an age.

Aelinor heals by revealing what the wound means. She is gentle to the broken and
merciless to comforting falsehood. Her servants may mend flesh, expose poison,
or force a court to look at the truth it buried.

Skarn is the sacred hunger behind envy, greed, and wrath. He offers no apology
for wanting more. He is dangerous because he can make ambition honest before he
makes it ruinous.

Vessimir stands where magic and truth meet. A mage who lies about cost, source,
or risk offends him more deeply than a mage who fails. His glass does not only
show what is present; it shows what was omitted.

Whisanne guards roads, songs, courage, and hard-won wisdom. She favors the
traveler who acts before certainty, the bard who preserves inconvenient memory,
and the reckless heart that survives long enough to learn.

Orthuun gives law a face that does not blink. His judgment is not kindness, but
it is meant to outlast rage. Priests who serve him must be able to carry an
unpopular sentence without turning it into cruelty.

Mphirae keeps the last door and the hush around names. She is neither cruel nor
kind. Her servants guard graves, secrets, final words, and the boundary that
keeps permanent death meaningful.

## Priest Empowerment

Priests seek empowerment from one god through roleplay-proven service, public
conduct, sacrifice, and doctrine alignment. Empowerment can unlock
Supplications, visible favor, faction standing, or divine tasks. It can also be
withheld or revoked.

Every empowerment record should name the god, witnessed conduct, granted power,
revocation risk, and current embodiment source: AI, human Immortal, or co-embodied. No test or tool may treat priesthood as a silent skill purchase.

## Faction Rule

Factions are political engines. Each faction needs a doctrine, gate, item of power, shrine or seat, leader title, roleplay advancement path, and reason to
ally or betray. They are AI-led by default, can be influenced by human Immortal
embodiment, and advance members through measured roleplay quality.

Faction content is authored in the LitD editor and rendered by the proprietary
Rust/wgpu engine. Do not describe current implementation as g3n.

## Faction Details

The Ironbound gather around anvils, scars, and weapons that remember every spell
they broke. Their best members are disciplined, not merely angry. The Anvil-Heart
should make anti-magic action concrete: resistance, disruption, oath-forged
arms, and public proof that mortal steel still matters.

The Dawnward is an order of banners, lamps, shields, and witnesses. They fight
the Hollow in public because corruption thrives in private. The Unfaltering Lamp
should expose hidden wrongness, steady morale, and make cowardice visible.

The Vesper Court records, performs, edits, and weaponizes memory. Their songs
can crown a hero or erase a traitor by never naming them again. The Echo Bell
should call memories into play: eulogies, rumors, reputations, and public proof.

The Iron Verdict turns law into marching armor. Its courts are fortified because
judgment without force is only advice. The Sealed Writ should authorize warrants,
sentences, extradition, and lawful pursuit across fractured lands.

The Onyx Dominion is empire made ritual. It offers order, roads, protection, and
a throne, then demands obedience as the price. The Onyx Crown should make the
Sovereign's authority visible and dangerous, including player-earned imperial
command.

The Thornwild is not gentle nature. It is root-law, wolf patience, flooded
roads, and old trees judging stone walls. The Seedstone should make living
terrain, beasts, growth, and anti-city pressure into faction power.

The Hollow Pact is scholarship turned bargain. Its members know the cost is
real and call that cost a ladder. The Rift-Shard should open forbidden options
while recording debt, corruption, exposure, and reasons for every other faction
to fear them.

## Item-Of-Power Warfare

Each faction item must be physically meaningful in play. A rival who steals an
item and brings it to the correct shrine can strip or suppress faction powers
until the item is recovered. Defending an item should require patrols, politics,
spies, roleplay, and combat rather than a hidden invulnerable container.

Item warfare creates diplomacy. The Ironbound may ally with the Dawnward against
the Hollow Pact, then betray the Dawnward over a captured lamp. The Vesper Court
may make that betrayal immortal by choosing which song survives.

## Forbidden Drift

Do not make gods interchangeable buff vendors. Do not make priests buy
Supplication from trainers. Do not make factions only reputation tracks. Do not
make items of power cosmetic. Do not let alignment gates replace doctrine. Do
not copy stale engine wording from older issue bodies.
