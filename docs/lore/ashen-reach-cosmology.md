# Ashen Reach Cosmology

Canonical detail for issue #1056 under the #1055 Ashen Reach world bible. This
document defines Vhael's metaphysics, the Outer Dark, the nature of magic, the
Sundering, and the source-of-truth language future editor tools must use when
generating cosmology-facing lore.

## Originality Boundary

This is all-original LitD setting expression. The idea of a magical world, a
dangerous outer dark, divine favors, elemental spellcraft, forbidden bargains,
and permanent death are generic genre or game structures. The protected
expression here is ours: Vhael, the Weave, the Hollow, the Sundering, the Ashen
Reach, the rites, names, history, costs, factions, and written prose.

Do not copy protected setting text, maps, characters, named metaphysics, spell
lists, planes, or visual presentation from another fantasy world. If outside
references are useful, reduce them to generic design pressure only, then author
new Vhael-specific expression.

## The Two Truths

Vhael hangs between two truths.

The Weave is living order. It is not a place, god, or mana pool. It is the
pattern by which breath stays breath, stone keeps weight, names carry memory,
oaths can bind, and law can outlast the hand that wrote it. Every living thing
touches it, but most mortals feel it only as instinct, conscience, pattern, or
dread.

The Hollow is the Outer Dark. It is not empty space; it is the outside of shape.
There, hunger has no body until it borrows one, promises arrive before the mouth
that speaks them, and every bargain tries to make the world less fixed. Hollow
entities should never feel like ordinary monsters with foreign addresses. They
are wrongness pressing against the idea that a world can remain a world.

## Magic Is Reaching

Magic is the act of reaching. A caster reaches into the Weave to draw a thread
tight, reaches upward for a god who may answer, or reaches sideways into the
Hollow and lets something answer first.

Good magic is not safe magic. It may be lawful, holy, restrained, or witnessed,
but it still bends the pattern of the world. The best mages in Vhael are feared
because they understand how little pressure is needed to change a life forever.

## The Three Founts

| Fount | Source | Cost | Play Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Invocation | Mortal force drawn through the Weave. | Will, fatigue, miscast risk, witnessed responsibility. | Mage classes and elemental spellcraft. |
| Supplication | Power lent by a god to a proven servant. | Service, conduct, sacrifice, and revocation risk. | Priest empowerment and divine favors. |
| Pact | Power bought from a Hollow denizen. | Hidden debt, corruption, social danger, and shape-loss. | Hollow Pact powers and forbidden progression. |

No fount is a fallback for another. Invocation cannot fake priesthood,
Supplication cannot be stolen like a spellbook, and Pact power must never
quietly degrade into harmless flavor.

## Invocation

Invocation draws the seven currents through mortal discipline:

| Current | Shape | Typical Use |
|---|---|---|
| Fire | Heat, courage, hunger, ignition. | Battle magic, forging rites, signal flames. |
| Stone | Weight, endurance, walls, pressure. | Wards, barriers, slow violence, oath-stones. |
| Air | Motion, speech, distance, breath. | Travel, sound, evasion, messenger craft. |
| Tide | Flow, blood, exchange, erosion. | Healing-adjacent craft, water, cleansing, attrition. |
| Frost | Stillness, preservation, silence. | Binding, slowing, tomb-wards, memory vaults. |
| Mire | Rot, growth, concealment, bog-law. | Poisons, roots, traps, patient transformation. |
| Storm | Shock, omen, rupture, sudden judgment. | Lightning, battlefield control, sky signs. |

The currents are not seven schools with neat borders. Strong invokers can braid
them, but every braid should carry a legible cost and a visible source of power.
The Weave dislikes waste; flashy magic that changes nothing should feel vulgar
inside Vhael's fiction.

## Supplication

Supplication is power lent by a god. A priest is not empowered because a menu
was completed; a priest is empowered because their conduct becomes legible to a
divine will. Korthac may answer courage, Aelinor may answer mercy joined to
truth, Orthuun may answer lawful dedication, and Mphirae may answer reverent
silence at the edge of death.

Divine power has memory. It can be revoked, withheld, redirected, or made
public. A false servant may still speak prayers, but the Weave does not bend for
borrowed holiness without the lender's consent.

## Pact

Pact magic is the forbidden third path. A Hollow denizen offers a complete
answer: a wound closed, a rival broken, a secret returned, a fear removed. The
price is never fully visible when the bargain is made.

Pact costs must be concrete in play and story. A pact can mark the body, thin a
name on the Mourning Wall, make a character easier for Hollow things to find,
or give a faction reason to hunt the bearer. The Hollow Pact faction knows this
and calls the cost a ladder.

## Death And Memory

When a body fails, the Weave does not re-knit the thread. It remembers it.
Resurrection is not normal cosmology in Vhael. The dead return only as memory,
monument, story, consequence, or Hollow-tainted wrongness.

This is why the oldest greeting remains: "by what name will you be mourned?"
The question is metaphysics, social pressure, and game thesis at once. A life
lived loudly leaves a brighter scar. A life erased by cowardice, secrecy, or
forgotten violence becomes easier for the Hollow to misname.

## The Sundering

The Sundering began when pact-makers tried to open a controlled seam into the
Hollow and discovered that the outside does not stay where it is placed. For a
generation the dead walked, bells rang without hands, shadows moved against the
sun, and entire provinces lost their original names.

The gods spent themselves sealing the wound. They did not restore the world;
they stopped it from becoming less than world. The Ashen Reach is the remaining
scar: grey, cold, half-real, and crossed by cyan fractures where the Weave shows
through under stress.

The Reach draws adventurers because thin Weave makes power cheaper and danger
closer. Relics surface there, Hollow things press there, and reputations are
either carved into history or scattered into ash.

## Forbidden Drift

Do not introduce normal resurrection as a convenience feature. Do not make Pact
power harmless. Do not let Supplication become a skill-trainer purchase. Do not
make the Hollow a simple elemental realm. Do not make the Weave a fuel bar. Do
not rename the Sundering scar into a borrowed apocalypse trope.
